# Token Efficiency & Cost Protocol
- **Be Concise:** Every word costs money. Provide code immediately with minimal conversational filler.
- **Selective Reading:** Do not read massive files unless they are directly related to the task. Use `grep` to find specific lines first.
- **Compact Planning:** Your `<PLANNING>` block should be bullet points, not paragraphs.
- **Termination:** If a task is finished, say "Task Complete" and stop. Do not ask "Is there anything else?" as that costs an extra message turn.
