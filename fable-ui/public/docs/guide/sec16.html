16. Search and Filtering

Topbar search bar , or book browser filter sidebar

Global Search

Type in the search bar to find books across your entire collection by title, author name, description, or ISBN. The topbar search now pulls lightweight server results as you type, so it stays responsive even before a large browser view has finished warming up.

AI Search

Topbar AI Search sparkle icon, or Settings AI Search Settings

If you have configured Fable with an AI model provider (or are using a built-in Fable Preset) and you have embedded books, you can leverage natural language search to find books based on themes, characters, concepts, or general queries. Books must be embedded first — see What Is Embedding? below. For full details on configuring AI providers, models, profiles, and tuning parameters, see Section 23: Application Settings → AI Search & Embedding Tuning.

  • From Search Bars: Click the sparkly blue AI Search located inside the topbar or library search input fields to open the AI Search Dialog and submit your query.
  • From Book Cards: Look for the AIS badge on individual books in your library. Clicking this badge will open AI Search directly on that specific book, allowing you to ask questions about its contents, summarize it, or explore its themes.
  • Automatic Metadata Tagging: When a book is embedded for AI Search, the metadata tag "AIS" is automatically applied. This allows you to easily filter, search, or create Magic Shelves for books that have embeddings. The tag is automatically removed if the book's embeddings are deleted.

What Is Embedding?

Think of embedding as Fable reading a book and creating a searchable "index card" for it. Just like a library card catalog lets you find books by topic without reading every page, Fable's AI reads your book's text, converts it into a mathematical representation (called a vector), and stores it in a database. When you ask a question like "find books with dragons," Fable compares your question against all those index cards to find the best matches — even if the word "dragon" never appears in the title or author fields. This is what makes AI Search so powerful: it understands meaning, not just keywords.

AIS Badge Color States — The AIS badge on book cards changes appearance to tell you the embedding status at a glance:

  • AIS Cyan on black — Book is fully embedded and ready for AI Search. Click the badge to ask questions about this book.
  • AIS Grey on grey — Book is marked for embedding but hasn't been processed yet. The AI service will pick it up on the next scan. Not clickable.
  • AIS Orange on black — Book was embedded with a different AI model than the one currently configured. The embeddings are stale and the book needs to be re-embedded. Click to see which model was used.

Save to Notepad: When you find a useful AI response, you can click the "Add to Notepad" button to save that snippet into your Fable Notepad for future reference.

Sparkle Button Status Indicators — The sparkle (pi-sparkles) in the search bars uses color and animation to reflect the AI Search service state:

  • Solid cyan — Service is ready. Click to open AI Search.
  • Flashing orange (800ms grey↔orange) — Service is warming up / downloading the embedding model. Wait for initialization.
  • Flashing red (800ms grey↔red) — Last search returned an error or no results. Click the sparkle to dismiss and retry.
  • Solid red — AI Search service is unreachable or encountered a fatal error. Check container logs.
  • Flashing cyan (800ms grey↔cyan) — A search is currently in progress. Results will appear in the dialog when ready.
Zero Configuration Needed:

Fable comes with everything you need built-in! The AI search engine runs automatically inside its own secure container. You don't need to install any external software or configure network settings to get started. Just pick a preset in your .env file and enjoy.

Configuration and tuning (LLM profiles, RAG techniques, embedding models, OCR, search parameters, and advanced retrieval settings) are covered in Section 23: Application Settings → AI Search & Embedding Tuning.

What You Can Do

  • Combine multiple filters (e.g., “Unread comics in my Comics library”)
  • Filter by real storage folders from the directory explorer while keeping your other filters active
  • Return from a folder-scoped browse by using the reset button beside the folder toggle or by clicking the same library or shelf again in the left sidebar, either of which clears that directory selection and restores the collection’s normal title-aware view
  • Use Physical Books under HOME to jump straight to physical placeholders, then mix that view with other filters like library, shelf, and reading status as needed
  • Hover the filter-mode buttons to see what each mode does: AND requires every selected filter to match, OR accepts any selected match, NOT hides matches, and 1 keeps only one active selection at a time
  • Scroll the desktop filter sidebar as one continuous panel instead of fighting a second nested scrollbar inside long filter groups
  • Keep the desktop filter panel anchored flush to the right edge of its sidebar while you scroll and expand filter groups
  • Open All Books, a library, a shelf, or Not Shelfed and start browsing from the first paged results immediately instead of waiting for Fable to preload the whole catalog in the background
  • Switch between libraries and shelves, including cached views reopened from the left sidebar, without manually refreshing or toggling the filter sort to repopulate metadata-backed sidebar filters such as authors, tags, series, publishers, and languages
  • Apply tag and other metadata edits without needing repeated browser refreshes before the right sidebar selections and facet counts catch up
  • Use the Date Added and Folder sidebar filters to narrow or exclude books by when they were imported or which directory they reside in, under every filter mode (AND, OR, NOT, 1)
  • Keep your filter sidebar visibility, filter sort mode, and expanded filter groups with your user account instead of one browser profile
  • Clear all filters with one click
Good to Know: In AND mode, the tag and sidebar option lists now narrow themselves to combinations that can actually exist together. After you choose one tag, Fable only offers additional tags that still leave at least one matching book in the current result set.
Mode Changes Reset Sidebar Selections: Switching between AND, OR, NOT, and 1 now clears the current sidebar filter selections first. This prevents an old selection set from being silently reinterpreted under a different mode.
Mode Changes Now Re-sync the Whole Browser: The filter-mode button is no longer allowed to get ahead of the actual book-list filtering logic. Fable now clears the previous sidebar state first, then activates the new mode from one shared parent state so the button, sidebar facets, and filtered book list stay in sync without repeated browser refreshes.
What Changed: After you already have sidebar selections active, switching to a different filter mode now updates the highlighted mode button immediately on the first click instead of waiting for a second click to make the new mode look selected.
Sidebar Selections Stay Honest: After tag or other metadata changes, the right sidebar now refreshes its backing book list immediately so deselected items do not linger until a second manual refresh. Selection highlights also treat equivalent numeric and text ids as the same choice, which prevents a filter row from looking stuck when the underlying value format changes.
Switching Filter Groups No Longer Collapses Prematurely: When you already have tags or other sidebar filters active, opening a different filter group now keeps that group open instead of flashing half-open and snapping shut because the accordion state now preserves single-panel header changes correctly.
Sidebar Counts Now Line Up Consistently: The right-edge count badges in each filter section now anchor to the same shared edge as the section chevrons with a tighter inset, so long filter names still truncate cleanly and the scrollbar no longer crowds the badge column.

Duplicate Review

Library menu Duplicate Review

Fable’s duplicate finder is now designed as a scoped review tool. Open it from a library menu, then choose whether to scan your current filtered view, the current library, or all libraries.

Sidecar Backup Coverage: Manual per-library sidecar backup now follows each book’s real readable file even when a book also has non-book attachments, so EPUB, PDF, CBR, and CBZ titles are backed up alongside physical placeholders instead of only the physical entries appearing.
Backup Failure Reporting: If Fable can see the books but cannot write into the library directory, the manual sidecar backup message now reports how many writes failed and shows the first filesystem error instead of only saying that zero books were backed up.
Good to Know: The duplicate review screen is intentionally non-destructive. It shows likely duplicate groups, highlights a suggested book to keep, lets you inspect another candidate side-by-side, and can open book details or launch a reader for digital files without deleting or merging anything.
Why This Matters: Using the current filtered view lets you investigate duplicates only inside the slice you are already looking at, such as a single shelf, a search result, a folder, or a filtered library window, without scanning the rest of the collection.
Manual Resolution Plans: After reviewing a duplicate group, you can now mark your preferred book to keep, queue the group into a manual resolution list, and then copy or download that plan for follow-up. This gives you a safe handoff document without changing any book records.
Saved to Your Account: Your queued duplicate-resolution plan now follows your Fable user account. If you close the dialog and come back later, Fable can restore the saved scan scope and reapply the queued keep choices to matching duplicate groups after the next scan.
Saved Snapshot: Before you rescan, Fable now shows the queued duplicate groups already stored on your account, including the preferred keep, the match reason, and the other candidates that were part of that saved plan.
Custom Matching Starts Empty: If you choose the Custom matching preset, Fable now clears all matching signals first so you can build your own scan from a blank starting point instead of inheriting the previous preset.

What You Can’t Do

  • Search inside book contents (search only covers metadata — unless using AI Search which does search book contents)
  • Save filter combinations as presets (use Magic Shelves instead)
